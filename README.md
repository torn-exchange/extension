# Torn Exchange Helper for Traders

Adds a Torn Exchange price lookup and receipt tool directly to your Torn trade page. No more tabbing back and forth to Torn Exchange to price a trade or generate a receipt.

## Features

- One-click price lookup for every item in the current trade, pulled straight from your Torn Exchange price list
- Editable price table with live profit and total calculation as you adjust prices
- Submit the trade to generate a Torn Exchange receipt, with a shareable receipt link and a ready-to-send trade message
- Copy the total or trade message to your clipboard with one click
- Automatically detects if a trade already has a receipt (even after reloading the page) and shows it instead of making you start over
- Resubmit support if trade details change after a receipt was created

## Setup

On first use, you'll be asked for your Torn API key (the same one you use to log into Torn Exchange) — this is stored locally by the script and only sent to Torn Exchange to look up your receipts.

## Requirements

Requires an active Torn Exchange account with a price list.
